Golden sweeps, with a real track record

The most aggressive footprint in the tape — swept across exchanges, paying up for urgency.

A golden sweep is an order large and urgent enough to sweep multiple exchanges and pay the offer. It's the closest thing options flow has to a conviction tell. What makes a sweep 'golden'

  • Multi-exchange execution — filled across venues in a hurry.
  • Aggressor paying the ask — urgency, not patience.
  • Size relative to the contract's normal liquidity.

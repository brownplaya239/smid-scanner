Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

TickerDesk ("we", "us", "the service") provides a stock research dashboard at tickerdesk.io. This policy explains what data we collect from you, how we use it, and what controls you have. We've kept it short and human-readable on purpose.

What we collect

  • Account info — your email address (required for sign-in) and an optional display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and Google account email; we do not receive your Google password, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data.
  • Your dashboard activity — tickers you add to your watchlist, weights you assign them, and trade journal entries you log. All of this is stored only against your account and visible only to you.
  • Research reports you request — the ticker, when you asked, and the finished PDF, stored privately against your account. We count them to apply your plan's report limit.
  • What you send to AI features — a portfolio screenshot you upload for import, or a question you type into the Trade Desk "Ask" box. These are sent to Anthropic to process (see below); we don't keep the screenshot.
  • Subscription status — if you pay, your plan, its renewal date and your Stripe customer ID. Your card details stay with Stripe.
  • Notification subscriptions — if you enable browser push notifications or the Daily Brief email, we store the technical details needed to deliver them (push endpoint and encryption keys for browser; email address for the brief).
  • Basic technical metadata — browser user-agent string and device label (e.g. "Chrome on Windows") attached to each push subscription, so you can manage multiple devices.
  • Visitor analytics — to learn which pages and links bring people to TickerDesk and where they stop, we count a small set of steps: a page view (the page, the site that sent you, and any utm_ campaign tag in the link), signing up for the Daily Brief, creating an account, requesting a report, clicking an upgrade button, starting checkout, and starting or cancelling a paid plan. If you are signed in, these are linked to your TickerDesk user ID, never your email or name. We do not record your screen, what you type, or what you click beyond those steps.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect or store passwords. Sign-in is exclusively via email magic-link or Google OAuth — your credentials live with the auth provider, not us.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number. We do not collect bank accounts, brokerage credentials or social security numbers, and we do not connect to your broker.
  • We do not track you across the web. There are no advertising scripts or advertising cookies on TickerDesk. Our one analytics tool (PostHog, below) sets no cookies and forgets a signed-out visitor when the browser tab closes.
  • If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, our analytics does not load in your browser at all. If you subscribe or cancel, our server still records that the subscription started or ended (with your account id and plan, no email or name), because that is part of running billing.

How we use your data

  • To render your personalized dashboard (My Desk, Watchlist tab, Trade Log).
  • To send the Daily Brief email if you've opted in, and the push notifications if you've enabled them.
  • To debug failures (we log delivery attempts for emails and pushes so we can tell if a push endpoint expired or an email bounced).
  • To understand, in aggregate, which channels bring visitors and how many go on to sign up or subscribe, so we know what to improve. Analytics is never used for advertising.

Third parties we use

These services process data on our behalf, only to run the features described here. Most of the app's requests go through our Cloudflare worker, but your browser also connects directly to Supabase (sign-in and your saved data), TradingView (the Charts tab), unpkg (a charting library file) and PostHog (analytics), so those services see your IP address too.

  • Supabase — hosts our database, file storage for your private reports, and user authentication. Your account, watchlist, journal, notes and reports live here. Your browser connects to it directly, so it sees your IP address.
  • Stripe — handles checkout, billing and the billing portal. Stripe receives your email address, your TickerDesk user ID and your payment details. We never see your full card number.
  • Anthropic — the AI model behind three features: reading a portfolio screenshot you upload, answering a question you type in the Trade Desk "Ask" box, and summarizing public SEC filings. It receives the screenshot or question you send, not your email or name. Research report PDFs are not written by AI.
  • GitHub — hosts the website and runs the jobs that build research reports. When you request a report, GitHub receives the ticker and your TickerDesk user ID (not your email) so the PDF can be filed to your account.
  • Cloudflare — provides DNS, CDN, data storage for published reports, and the edge worker that most of the app's requests pass through (not the direct connections to Supabase, TradingView and unpkg listed here). They see your IP address as the requester, as any CDN does.
  • TradingView — the interactive chart on the Charts tab is TradingView's widget, loaded straight from TradingView into your browser. TradingView receives the symbol you chart and your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies to that widget.
  • Yahoo Finance — price history for some features. Our worker asks Yahoo for the ticker symbols you look up; Yahoo receives the symbols, not your identity or IP address.
  • unpkg — a public file host that serves one charting library to your browser. It sees your IP address, nothing else.
  • Resend — delivers email (magic-link sign-in and the Daily Brief). It receives your email address and the message.
  • Polygon.io (Massive) — market data (quotes, candles, options flow, news). They receive the ticker symbols you look up, not your identity.
  • Finnhub — market news and analyst estimates. They receive ticker symbols (for example the one you request a report on), not your identity.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google. Google sees that you signed into TickerDesk.
  • PostHog — product analytics, hosted in the United States. Your browser loads PostHog's script and sends it the steps listed under "Visitor analytics" above; our worker also tells PostHog when a paid plan starts or ends, using only your TickerDesk user ID and the plan name. It sets no cookies: a signed-out visitor gets a random ID kept in the tab's session storage, which the browser deletes when the tab closes. Session recording, automatic click capture and form capture are switched off, and sign-in tokens are removed from page addresses before anything is sent. PostHog sees your IP address, as any service your browser connects to does.

We do not sell your data to anyone. We do not share your data with any party beyond what is strictly required to render the service.

How long we keep your data

We retain your account and watchlist for as long as your account is active. Notification delivery logs are kept indefinitely (they're small and useful for debugging). If you delete your account, all your data is permanently erased within 30 days.

Your rights

  • Access — every byte of your data is visible on your dashboard. Use the Watchlist tab and your account settings to review.
  • Export — email us and we'll send you a JSON export of everything we hold on you within 7 days.
  • Delete — email us at the address below to delete your account and all associated data. Or unsubscribe individual streams (Daily Brief, push) from the dashboard toggles.
  • Opt out of communications — every Daily Brief includes an unsubscribe option; the push notification toggle disables real-time alerts immediately.

Security

Data in our database is protected by row-level security, so other users can't read your watchlist, journal, notes or reports. Only TickerDesk's back-end accesses your data, to run the features you use. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS / TLS). We use only services with industry-standard security postures.

That said, no system is perfectly secure. Don't put information in TickerDesk that you couldn't tolerate becoming public if we had a catastrophic breach.

Children

TickerDesk is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be announced via the Daily Brief or an in-dashboard banner.

Contact

Privacy questions, data export, account deletion: privacy@tickerdesk.io. We respond within 7 days.

Earnings options flow and trade ideas

What the options are pricing into the print — versus what the stock has actually done.

The interesting earnings edge isn't the calendar, it's the mispricing. TickerDesk compares each name's implied move to its historically realized move, measures post-report drift, reads the options flow going into the print, and turns it into rule-based, self-graded trade ideas for names with real size.

Idea types

  • Momentum into print — leaders with bullish flow and positive historical post-report drift.
  • Post-report drift — the after-the-print trade, no binary overnight risk.
  • Vol rich / vol cheap — implied move well above or below the realized history.
  • Binary caution — big typical mover already extended into the event.

Ideas are capped to $1B+ market caps and every type carries its own graded track record. Nothing publishes a hit rate before it has 30 real graded outcomes.

Frequently asked

Does it tell me to buy earnings?

No. It surfaces where options pricing diverges from history and frames defined-risk ideas. It's educational, not advice, and flags crowded binaries to avoid.

Which names are covered?

Large, liquid names reporting within about a week; the trade-idea layer is floored at $1B market cap.

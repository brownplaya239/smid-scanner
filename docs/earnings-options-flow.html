Earnings options flow and graded setups

What the options are pricing into the print — versus what the stock has actually done.

The calendar tells you when a company reports; it doesn't tell you how options are priced. TickerDesk compares each name's implied move to its historically realized move, measures post-report drift, reads the options flow going into the print, and sorts names into rule-based setup types for names with real size. Each type is graded in public, including when it fails.

Setup types

  • Momentum into print — leaders with bullish flow and positive historical post-report drift.
  • Post-report drift — how the stock has tended to move in the days after past reports.
  • Vol rich / vol cheap — implied move well above or below the realized history.
  • Binary caution — big typical mover already extended into the event.

Setups are capped to $1B+ market caps and every type carries its own graded track record. Nothing publishes a hit rate before it has 30 real graded outcomes.

Frequently asked

Does it tell me to buy earnings?

No. It shows where options pricing differs from history and labels crowded, extended names. It's educational, not advice.

Which names are covered?

Large, liquid names reporting within about a week; the setup layer is floored at $1B market cap.
